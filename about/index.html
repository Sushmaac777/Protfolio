About me

Every opportunity I've earned began with learning how to adapt.

My journey into software engineering wasn't defined by shortcuts or perfect conditions. Growing up with a full Nepali-medium education meant that every new academic stage demanded extra effort, especially while transitioning into science and engineering. Rather than seeing those challenges as limitations, they became the foundation of how I approach learning today: patiently, consistently, and with curiosity.

Those experiences eventually led me toward computer engineering, academic research, backend development, and now quality assurance, where I enjoy understanding how software behaves, uncovering hidden issues, and helping build systems people can rely on.
